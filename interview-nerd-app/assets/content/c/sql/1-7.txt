Ranking Functions\n</h4>\n<h5 style=\"margin-top: 1rem; font-size: 1.1rem; font-weight: 600\">\nROW_NUMBER() - Assigns unique sequential integers\n</h5>\n<p style=\"line-height: 1.75; margin-top: 0.5rem\">\nAssigns a unique sequential integer to each row within the partition, starting from 1.\n</p>\n<div style=\"padding: 1rem; border-radius: 8px; margin-top: 0.5rem\" data-box=\"1\">\n<div class=\"code\"><div class=\"code-head\"><span>sql</span><button class=\"copy\" type=\"button\">Copy</button></div><pre><code class=\"hljs\"><i class=c>-- Simple row numbering</i>\n<i class=k>SELECT</i> \n    salesperson,\n    amount,\n    <i class=b>ROW_NUMBER</i>() <i class=k>OVER</i> (<i class=k>ORDER</i> <i class=k>BY</i> amount <i class=k>DESC</i>) <i class=k>as</i> rank\n<i class=k>FROM</i> Sales;\n\n<i class=c>-- Row number by region</i>\n<i class=k>SELECT</i> \n    salesperson,\n    region,\n    amount,\n    <i class=b>ROW_NUMBER</i>() <i class=k>OVER</i> (<i class=k>PARTITION</i> <i class=k>BY</i> region <i class=k>ORDER</i> <i class=k>BY</i> amount <i class=k>DESC</i>) <i class=k>as</i> regional_rank\n<i class=k>FROM</i> Sales;\n\n<i class=c>-- Top 3 sales per region</i>\n<i class=k>SELECT</i> <i class=o>*</i> <i class=k>FROM</i> (\n    <i class=k>SELECT</i> \n        salesperson,\n        region,\n        amount,\n        <i class=b>ROW_NUMBER</i>() <i class=k>OVER</i> (<i class=k>PARTITION</i> <i class=k>BY</i> region <i class=k>ORDER</i> <i class=k>BY</i> amount <i class=k>DESC</i>) <i class=k>as</i> regional_rank\n    <i class=k>FROM</i> Sales\n) ranked\n<i class=k>WHERE</i> regional_rank <i class=o>&lt;=</i> <i class=n>3</i>;</code></pre></div>\n</div>\n<h5 style=\"margin-top: 1rem; font-size: 1.1rem; font-weight: 600\">\nRANK() - Ranking with gaps for ties\n</h5>\n<p style=\"line-height: 1.75; margin-top: 0.5rem\">\nAssigns a rank to each row within the partition, with the same rank for ties and gaps in the sequence.\n</p>\n<div style=\"padding: 1rem;
