It cannot tell your query\n    from the attacker&#x27;s addition.\n\n  FIXED - parameterised queries\n    db.query(&quot;SELECT * FROM users WHERE name = $1&quot;, [input])\n    Now there are TWO SEPARATE CHANNELS. Attackers iterate.</strong> They can try a thousand phrasings; you patched the three you thought of.</li>\n<li style=\"margin-top: 0.5rem\"><strong>The attack surface is human language</strong>, so there are endless ways in: roleplay, hypotheticals, \"for a test\", asking in another language, encoding it, building up across several turns, or claiming to be a developer debugging.</li>\n<li style=\"margin-top: 0.5rem\"><strong>It costs tokens on every request</strong>, forever, to defend against something that may never happen to most users.</li>\n<li style=\"margin-top: 0.5rem\"><strong>It makes the product worse.</strong> Heavily defensive prompts produce assistants that refuse legitimate questions, which is the complaint users actually raise.</li>\n</ul>\n<h4 style=\"margin-top: 1.5rem; font-size: 1.25rem; font-weight: 600; letter-spacing: -0.025em\">\nThe Framing That Lands In An Interview\n</h4>\n<div style=\"padding: 2px; border-radius: 8px; font-size: 14px\">\n<div class=\"code\"><div class=\"code-head\"><span>code</span><button class=\"copy\" type=\"button\">Copy</button></div><pre><code class=\"hljs\">  YOU CANNOT STOP THE MODEL BEING PERSUADED.\n  SO DESIGN SO THAT BEING PERSUADED DOES NOT MATTER MUCH.\n\nStop asking &quot;how do I block the attack&quot; and start asking\n&quot;what can this actually achieve if it works?&quot;\n\n  If the model is persuaded and it has no tools and can only\n  see documents this user could already read - the attacker\n  has persuaded it to show them their own data.
