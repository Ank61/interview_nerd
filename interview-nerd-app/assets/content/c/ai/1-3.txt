Human raters consistently prefer a confident answer to \"I don't know\", so preference tuning teaches the model that producing <em>something</em> scores better than abstaining. There is also no internal \"not found\" signal — the model always has a probability distribution over next tokens and always samples from it.\n</p>\n<p style=\"line-height: 1.75; margin-top: 1.0rem\">\nSo abstention is <strong>behaviour you have to actively construct</strong>, working against the model's training.
