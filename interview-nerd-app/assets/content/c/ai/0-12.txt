It has <strong>no scratchpad</strong> — no hidden place to work things out. The only place it can \"think\" is in the tokens it produces.\n</p>\n<p style=\"line-height: 1.75; margin-top: 1.0rem\">\nSo if you demand the final answer immediately, you are asking it to do a five-step calculation in the space of one token. That is not enough room. If instead you let it write the intermediate steps, each step becomes tokens it can then read back and build on. <strong>The reasoning text is not an explanation of the computation — it is the computation.</strong>\n</p>\n<p style=\"line-height: 1.75; margin-top: 1.0rem\">\nA human analogy: try multiplying 47 by 68 in your head instantly versus working it out on paper. Same brain, different amount of working room.
