If you stream tokens to the user, you have already sent the beginning of the answer before you can check the whole thing. Three options, and none are free: <strong>buffer everything</strong> and lose the reason you were streaming; <strong>check in chunks</strong>, which works for pattern matching and not for anything needing the full answer; or <strong>stream and retract</strong>, which is a poor experience and does not un-see anything.\n</p>\n<p style=\"line-height: 1.75; margin-top: 1.0rem\">\nThe usual resolution: stream the text, but <strong>never stream an action</strong> — anything with a side effect waits for the complete output and the full checks.
