This is the cascade pattern — and remember the caveat: you now pay for both, so it only wins if the cheap model succeeds most of the time.\n</li>\n<li style=\"margin-top: 0.5rem\">\n<span style=\"font-weight: 600; font-size: 1.1rem\">Simplify the task :</span>\nIf a large schema keeps failing, split it into two smaller extractions. Return a clear error, fall back to a deterministic path, queue for human review — but do not return a half-validated object and hope. And surface it: a rising failure rate is a signal, not noise.\n</li>\n</ol>\n<h4 style=\"margin-top: 1.5rem; font-size: 1.25rem; font-weight: 600; letter-spacing: -0.025em\">\nWhen NOT To Retry\n</h4>\n<ul style=\"margin-top: 1.0rem; margin-left: 1.5rem; list-style-type: disc\">\n<li style=\"margin-top: 0.5rem\"><strong>A content-policy refusal.</strong> Retrying an identical request that was refused will be refused again, and repeatedly hammering a safety system is not a good look on your account.
