It answers from memory, that memory is compressed and out of date, and it cannot tell you which page it came from — so when it does not know, it produces a plausible guess rather than admitting the gap.\n</p>\n<p style=\"line-height: 1.75; margin-top: 1.0rem\">\nRAG turns it into an <strong>open-book exam</strong>. The relevant pages are on the desk. The model reads them and answers from them, and it can point at the paragraph it used. It is still the same student with the same reasoning ability — you have just stopped asking it to remember everything.\n</p>\n<h4 style=\"margin-top: 1.5rem; font-size: 1.25rem; font-weight: 600; letter-spacing: -0.025em\">\nThe Four Problems RAG Exists To Solve\n</h4>\n<ol style=\"margin-top: 1.5rem; margin-left: 1.5rem; list-style-type: number\">\n<li style=\"margin-top: 0.5rem\">\n<span style=\"font-weight: 600; font-size: 1.1rem\">It does not know your data :</span>\nYour policies, tickets, contracts and wiki were never in its training data. They are private. No amount of prompting reveals information the model never saw.\n</li>\n<li style=\"margin-top: 0.5rem\">\n<span style=\"font-weight: 600; font-size: 1.1rem\">Its knowledge is frozen :</span>\nEvery model has a knowledge cutoff. Anything after it does not exist as far as the model is concerned — and it has no way to know that your question falls after that date.\n</li>\n<li style=\"margin-top: 0.5rem\">\n<span style=\"font-weight: 600; font-size: 1.1rem\">It hallucinates when it does not know :</span>\nThere is no internal \"not found\" signal, so the most likely continuation of a question is a fluent answer whether or not one exists. Putting the real text in front of it converts an unanswerable factuality problem into a checkable faithfulness problem — which is the single most useful thing RAG does.\n</li>\n<li style=\"margin-top: 0.5rem\">\n<span style=\"font-weight: 600; font-size: 1.1rem\">It cannot cite anything :</span>\nA bare model cannot tell you where an answer came from, because the knowledge is smeared across billions of weights.
