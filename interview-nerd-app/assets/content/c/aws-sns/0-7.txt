One team's outage never becomes another team's incident.</li>\n<li><strong>Its own scaling.</strong> The analytics consumer might need fifty workers while the email consumer needs two. A <strong>queue policy</strong> is a permissions document attached to the SQS queue saying who is allowed to use it. By default only the queue's own account principals can send to it, and SNS is a different service acting on its own behalf. You must add a statement allowing the <code>sns.amazonaws.com</code> service principal to call <code>sqs:SendMessage</code>, restricted with a condition to your topic's ARN so no other topic can dump messages into your queue. This is common in larger organisations and it works cleanly: the topic's <strong>topic policy</strong> must allow the subscriber account to call <code>sns:Subscribe</code>, and the subscriber's <strong>queue policy</strong> must allow the topic's ARN to send.
