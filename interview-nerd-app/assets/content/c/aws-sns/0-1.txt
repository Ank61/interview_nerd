SNS holds a message only while it is delivering it. If a subscriber is unreachable, SNS retries according to the delivery policy — for HTTP/S that is a configurable series of immediate, pre-backoff, backoff and post-backoff retries over a bounded window; for SQS and Lambda, SNS retries internal failures on its own schedule.
