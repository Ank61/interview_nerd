Enumerate the ARNs.</li>\n</ul>\n<h4 style=\"margin-top: 1.5rem; font-size: 1.25rem; font-weight: 600\">Other touchpoints</h4>\n<ul style=\"margin-left: 1.5rem; list-style: disc; line-height: 2\">\n<li><strong>Logging and tracing</strong> — CloudWatch Logs delivery needs <code>logs:CreateLogDelivery</code>, <code>GetLogDelivery</code>, <code>UpdateLogDelivery</code>, <code>PutResourcePolicy</code> and <code>DescribeResourcePolicies</code>;
