It separates the construction of a complex object from its representation, allowing the same construction process to create different representations. This pattern is especially useful when creating objects that require many parameters or when the creation process involves multiple steps.\n</p>\n<h4 style=\"margin-top: 1.5rem; font-size: 1.25rem;
