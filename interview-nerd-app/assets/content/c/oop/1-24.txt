style=\"font-weight: 600; font-size: 1.1rem\">Protection Proxy:</span> Controls access rights to the original object based on permissions.\n</li>\n<li style=\"margin-top: 0.5rem\">\n<span 0.5rem\">\n<span style=\"font-weight: 600; font-size: 1.1rem\">Caching Proxy:</span> Stores results of expensive operations and returns cached results when appropriate.\n</li>\n</ol>\n<h4
