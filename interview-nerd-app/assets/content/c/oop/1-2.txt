1.1rem\">Lifecycle Dependency:</span> Child objects cannot exist without the parent.\n</li>\n<li style=\"margin-top: 0.5rem\">\n<span style=\"font-weight: 600; font-size: 1.1rem\">Exclusive is destroyed, children are automatically destroyed.\n</li>\n</ol>\n<h4 style=\"margin-top: 1.5rem; font-size: 1.25rem; font-weight: 600; letter-spacing: -0.025em\">\nAggregation (Weak \"Has-a\"
