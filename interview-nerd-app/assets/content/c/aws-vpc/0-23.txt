Capture ALL — a REJECT-only log tells you nothing about a successful intrusion.</li>\n<li><strong>Aggregation interval:</strong> 1 or 10 minutes. Records are aggregated and delivered with a delay, so Flow Logs are <em>near</em>-real-time, not live packet capture.</li>\n</ul>\n<h4 style=\"margin-top: 1.5rem; font-size: 1.25rem; font-weight: 600\">Reading a record</h4>\n<p style=\"line-height: 1.75\">The default (version 2) format is: <code>version account-id interface-id srcaddr dstaddr srcport dstport protocol packets bytes start end action log-status</code>. Two fields carry most of the value:</p>\n<ul style=\"margin-left: 1.5rem; list-style: disc; line-height: 2\">\n<li><strong><code>action</code></strong> — <code>ACCEPT</code> or <code>REJECT</code>, as decided by security groups and NACLs combined. Essential behind a NAT gateway, where <code>srcaddr</code> is the NAT and you want to know which instance actually sent it.</li>\n<li><strong><code>traffic-path</code></strong> — whether egress went via the IGW, a NAT gateway, a VPC peering connection, or a gateway endpoint. For payloads you need <strong>VPC Traffic Mirroring</strong>.</li>\n<li><strong>Why</strong> something was rejected.
