Your instance never sees its public IP: the ENI only holds a private address like <code>10.0.1.25</code>, and the IGW rewrites the source address to the associated public IP or Elastic IP on the way out, and rewrites the destination back to the private address on the way in. The <strong>egress-only internet gateway (EIGW)</strong> solves this: it is stateful and allows outbound IPv6 connections plus their replies, while blocking unsolicited inbound. It is <strong>free</strong>, unlike a NAT gateway.
