Context Trees and Cancellation Propagation</h4>\n<p style=\"line-height: 1.75\">\nContexts form a tree: every derived context has exactly one parent, and a parent can have many children (since you can call <code>WithCancel</code>, <code>WithTimeout</code>, and so on multiple times against the same parent context).
