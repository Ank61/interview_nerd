One-Way Data Binding vs Two-Way Binding\n</h4>\n<p style=\"line-height: 1.75\">\nFrameworks like older AngularJS popularized two-way data binding, where a change in the DOM (e.g. an\ninput) automatically updates a bound model, and vice versa, without explicit code.
