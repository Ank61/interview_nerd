Unlike Redux's centralized store model, Recoil uses a distributed graph of atoms (units of state) and selectors (derived state) that components can subscribe to independently. Built by Facebook, Recoil offers fine-grained reactivity, built-in async support, derived state computation, and seamless React integration through hooks.
