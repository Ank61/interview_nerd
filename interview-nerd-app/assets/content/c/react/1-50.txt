Middleware sits in the dispatch pipeline, intercepting every action before it reaches the reducer, enabling powerful capabilities like logging, crash reporting, asynchronous API calls, routing, and more. Understanding middleware is essential for handling side effects in Redux applications, as reducers must remain pure functions. Middleware can inspect, modify, delay, or even stop actions, dispatch additional actions, and access the store's state. Modern Redux development commonly uses middleware like Redux Thunk for async logic, custom middleware for logging and analytics, and specialized middleware for complex async flows.\n</p>\n<h4 style=\"margin-top: 1.5rem; font-size: 1.25rem; font-weight: 600; letter-spacing: -0.025em\">\n1.
