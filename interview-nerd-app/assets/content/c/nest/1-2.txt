Behind a load balancer, the connection your app sees comes from the balancer, so <code>req.ip</code> is the balancer's address and <code>req.protocol</code> says <code>http</code> even though the client used HTTPS.\n</p>\n<p style=\"line-height: 1.75; margin-top: 1.0rem\">\nThe visible result is that rate limiting blocks everyone at once, because the throttler sees all traffic coming from one IP. Without it, <code>onModuleDestroy</code> and <code>onApplicationShutdown</code> <span style=\"font-weight: 600\">never run</span>.
