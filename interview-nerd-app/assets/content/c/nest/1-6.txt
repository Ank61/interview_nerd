So:\n</p>\n<ul style=\"margin-top: 1.0rem; margin-left: 1.5rem; list-style-type: disc\">\n<li style=\"margin-top: 0.4rem\">The <span style=\"font-weight: 600\">first half</span> of every interceptor still runs normally &mdash; timers start, logs are written.</li>\n<li style=\"margin-top: 0.4rem\">The <span style=\"font-weight: 600\">second half</span> receives <code>undefined</code>, because there is no return value.</li>\n<li style=\"margin-top: 0.4rem\">Even if the interceptor produces something, Nest never writes it, because in that mode Nest is not writing the response.</li>\n</ul>\n<p style=\"line-height: 1.75; margin-top: 1.0rem\">\nSo <span style=\"font-weight: 600\">observing</span> interceptors keep working while <span style=\"font-weight: 600\">transforming</span> ones silently stop.
