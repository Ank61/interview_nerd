border-radius: 8px; font-size: 14px; margin-top: 1rem\">\n<div class=\"code\"><div class=\"code-head\"><span>bash</span><button class=\"copy\" type=\"button\">Copy</button></div><pre><code class=\"hljs\"><i class=c># Find mutual friends</i>\nSADD friends:alice <i class=s>&quot;bob&quot;</i> <i class=s>&quot;charlie&quot;</i> <i class=s>&quot;david&quot;</i>\nSADD friends:bob <i class=s>&quot;alice&quot;</i> <i class=s>&quot;charlie&quot;</i> <i class=s>&quot;eve&quot;</i>\n\n<i class=c># Get mutual friends between alice and bob</i>\nSINTER friends:alice friends:bob\n<i class=c># Returns: &quot;charlie&quot;</i>\n\n<i class=c># Store intersection result</i>\nSINTERSTORE mutual:alice:bob friends:alice friends:bob\n\n<i class=c># Multi-way intersection (users who have all tags)</i>\nSADD tag:redis <i class=s>&quot;user1&quot;</i> <i class=s>&quot;user2&quot;</i> <i class=s>&quot;user3&quot;</i>\nSADD tag:nosql <i class=s>&quot;user2&quot;</i> <i class=s>&quot;user3&quot;</i> <i class=s>&quot;user4&quot;</i>\nSADD tag:database <i class=s>&quot;user2&quot;</i> <i class=s>&quot;user5&quot;</i>\n\n<i class=c># Find users with all three tags</i>\nSINTER tag:redis tag:nosql tag:database\n<i class=c># Returns: &quot;user2&quot;</i>\n\n<i class=c># Get count without storing</i>\nSINTER tag:redis tag:nosql | SCARD  <i class=c># Won&#x27;t work directly</i>\n<i class=c># Better approach:</i>\nSINTERSTORE temp:result tag:redis tag:nosql\nSCARD temp:result\nDEL temp:result\n\n<i class=c># Intersection with cardinality (Redis 7.0+)</i>\nSINTERCARD 2 tag:redis tag:nosql</code></pre></div>\n</div>\n<h4 style=\"margin-top: 1.5rem; font-size: 1.25rem; font-weight: 600; letter-spacing: -0.025em\">\n3. Set Union (All Unique Members)\n</h4>\n<p style=\"line-height: 1.75; margin-top: 0.5rem\">\nUnion combines all unique members from multiple sets into one result. This is perfect for aggregating data, combining search results, or finding all items across multiple categories. Duplicate members appear only once in the result.\n</p>\n<p style=\"line-height: 1.75; margin-top: 0.5rem\">\n<span style=\"font-weight: 600\">Common Use Cases:</span> Combining search results, aggregating user lists, merging categories, total unique visitors, combining recommendation sources.\n</p>\n<div style=\"padding: 2px; border-radius: 8px; font-size: 14px; margin-top: 1rem\">\n<div class=\"code\"><div class=\"code-head\"><span>bash</span><button class=\"copy\" type=\"button\">Copy</button></div><pre><code class=\"hljs\"><i class=c># Combine all friends</i>\nSADD friends:alice <i class=s>&quot;bob&quot;</i> <i class=s>&quot;charlie&quot;</i>\nSADD friends:bob <i class=s>&quot;alice&quot;</i> <i class=s>&quot;david&quot;</i>\nSADD friends:charlie <i class=s>&quot;alice&quot;</i> <i class=s>&quot;eve&quot;</i>\n\n<i class=c># Get everyone in the social network</i>\nSUNION friends:alice friends:bob friends:charlie\n<i class=c># Returns: &quot;alice&quot;, &quot;bob&quot;, &quot;charlie&quot;, &quot;david&quot;, &quot;eve&quot;</i>\n\n<i class=c># Store union result</i>\nSUNIONSTORE network:all friends:alice friends:bob friends:charlie\n\n<i class=c># Combine multiple tag groups</i>\nSADD premium:<i class=b>users</i> <i class=s>&quot;user1&quot;</i> <i class=s>&quot;user2&quot;</i> <i class=s>&quot;user3&quot;</i>\nSADD trial:<i class=b>users</i> <i class=s>&quot;user4&quot;</i> <i class=s>&quot;user5&quot;</i>\nSADD free:<i class=b>users</i> <i class=s>&quot;user6&quot;</i> <i class=s>&quot;user7&quot;</i>\n\n<i class=c># Get all users regardless of tier</i>\nSUNION premium:<i class=b>users</i> trial:<i class=b>users</i> free:<i class=b>users</i>\n\n<i class=c># Aggregate daily visitors into weekly</i>\nSUNIONSTORE visitors:week:1 \\\n  visitors:day:1 visitors:day:2 visitors:day:3 \\\n  visitors:day:4 visitors:day:5 visitors:day:6 visitors:day:7\n\n<i class=c># Get total count</i>\nSCARD visitors:week:1</code></pre></div>\n</div>\n<h4 style=\"margin-top: 1.5rem; font-size: 1.25rem; font-weight: 600; letter-spacing: -0.025em\">\n4. This is useful for finding unique items, excluding certain groups, or identifying gaps in data coverage.\n</p>\n<p style=\"line-height: 1.75; margin-top: 0.5rem\">\n<span style=\"font-weight: border-radius: 8px; font-size: 14px; margin-top: 1rem\">\n<div class=\"code\"><div class=\"code-head\"><span>bash</span><button class=\"copy\" type=\"button\">Copy</button></div><pre><code &quot;eve&quot;</i>\n\n<i class=c># Store difference result</i>\nSDIFFSTORE unique:alice friends:alice friends:bob\n\n<i class=c># Exclude blocked users from recommendations</i>\nSADD 1.1rem\">TTL for temporary data:</span> Set expiration on sets used for temporary tracking (sessions, online users).\n</li>\n<li style=\"margin-top: 0.5rem\">\n<span style=\"font-weight: 600;
