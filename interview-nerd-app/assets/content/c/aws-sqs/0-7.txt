It is a queue attribute, and the clock starts when the message is sent.</p>\n<ul style=\"margin-left: 1.5rem; list-style: disc; line-height: 2\">\n<li><strong>Default: 4 days</strong> (345,600 seconds).</li>\n<li><strong>Range: 60 seconds to 14 days</strong> (1,209,600 seconds).</li>\n<li><strong>It applies to unconsumed messages.</strong> A message being retried repeatedly still ages against this same clock.</li>\n</ul>\n<p style=\"line-height: 1.75\">The consequence beginners miss: if your consumers are down for longer than the retention period, SQS <strong>silently deletes</strong> the backlog.
